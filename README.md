# GrowthLab

### Find the next growth idea worth testing.

**Give AI agents your product. Let them compete to grow it.**

GrowthLab is a simple, open-source SEO growth tool for landing pages,
onboarding and launch messaging.

Give it your product and one clear goal. GrowthLab creates three practical
ideas, shows what each idea changes, and helps you choose what to test next.

**SEO and growth work with evidence. No fake rankings. No fake traffic.**

## How it works

1. **Bring your product.** Use a local folder, a public website or a short product brief.
2. **Name the opportunity.** For example: “Help more developers understand our product.”
3. **Compare three ideas.** Each idea has a clear hypothesis, supporting evidence, a proposed change and local checks.
4. **Choose your next test.** Review the differences, export a private report or apply one selected change.

Your original product stays safe. A failed check stays visible. Nothing is
published automatically.

## Why GrowthLab?

Most AI marketing tools return suggestions. GrowthLab keeps the question, the
ideas, the evidence and the decision together.

- **Three clear options** instead of one vague answer.
- **Useful evidence** instead of invented proof.
- **Before-and-after review** instead of blind copy and paste.
- **Honest results** labelled as observed, estimated, simulated or untested.

## What you can use it for

- Find a clearer message for a landing page.
- Turn a real search question into a useful page idea.
- Compare headlines, sections, calls to action and onboarding paths.
- Check page structure, links, forms and visible proof before publishing.
- Keep a simple record of what you tried and why.

GrowthLab helps you decide what to test. It does not promise rankings, traffic,
signups or revenue.

## Try the demo

The bundled demo uses PatchKit, a fictional product. It shows two ideas that
pass their checks and one idea with a deliberate failure. The original product
stays untouched.

The demo needs no API key and no Docker. From the project folder:

```sh
cargo install --path . --bin growthlab --locked
growthlab demo
```

The demo opens a local dashboard and labels its results clearly:

**SIMULATED ideas · OBSERVED checks · UNTESTED SEO outcomes**

Read the [demo walkthrough](docs/demo.md) or the [full installation guide](docs/dashboard.md).

## Review a page for SEO

You can review one local HTML file without creating a battle:

```sh
growthlab seo-audit --html ./website/index.html --format markdown
```

This is a local, estimated review of page structure and clarity. It does not
fetch the web or claim search rankings.

## Keep your work private

GrowthLab runs locally by default. Reports are private unless you choose to
export them. It does not send messages, publish changes, change live billing or
analytics, or access credentials and customer data automatically.
