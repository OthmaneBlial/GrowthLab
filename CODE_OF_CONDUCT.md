# Community code of conduct

GrowthLab welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels.
Be respectful, give concrete technical feedback, and make room for different
views. Critique code and claims without attacking people.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, sexualized attention, disclosure of
private information, and repeated disruptive behavior are unacceptable.
Do not use this community to promote spam, fake reviews, deceptive marketing or
unauthorized outreach.

Maintainers may edit or remove disruptive content, restrict participation, or
ban contributors whose behavior harms the community. Enforcement should be
proportionate and respect the privacy of everyone involved.

Report harmful content with GitHub's reporting tools or privately to a maintainer
through an available GitHub channel. Do not repost private information publicly.
This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests and other
spaces maintained by the GrowthLab project.
